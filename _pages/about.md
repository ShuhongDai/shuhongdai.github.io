I am currently a Ph.D. student in the [Edge AI Group](https://edgeai-informatik.uibk.ac.at/) at the University of Innsbruck, working under the supervision of [Prof. Radu Prodan](https://edgeai-informatik.uibk.ac.at/team/raduprodan). My research focuses on distributed systems and agentic AI.

I received my Master's degree in Software Engineering in 2026. During my master's studies, I was supervised by [Prof. Long Cheng](https://longcheng.eu/) and conducted research in the [EPI Lab](https://energy-pi.com/en) led by him. My research mainly focused on multi-agent reinforcement learning for intelligent transportation systems. During this period, I also completed an internship at the AI Lab of the [CRRC Academy](https://www.crrcgc.cc/yjy/155_9922/index.html).

Before that, I received my Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering in 2023. During my undergraduate studies, my interests included power converters and embedded operating systems.


Outside of academia, I enjoy playing Go, Chinese chess, and chess; I have also reached Grandmaster rank in Teamfight Tactics for nine consecutive seasons.
